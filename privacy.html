What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We operate no servers that receive your data. The developer never sees your notes, because they never reach us.

storage02 / 09

Where your notes live

Your notebooks and notes are stored locally on your device. Lembaran keeps automatic on-device backups so it can recover your notes if a file is damaged.

If you choose to create a backup from Settings, the exported file is saved wherever you send it, for example your own iCloud Drive or Files. That copy is governed by Apple’s iCloud terms, not by us, and we have no access to it.

If your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes your Lembaran notes, the same as it does for your other apps. Apple handles those backups under its own terms, and we have no access to them.

You can lock single notes, labels and notebooks. Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode is checked by iOS on your device, and nothing about it is sent anywhere. A lock hides that content inside Lembaran, in Spotlight and Siri, and in widgets, and asks again before it is exported. It is a lock on the app, not extra encryption: locked notes are stored with the same iPhone protection as your other notes, and a backup or Markdown file you export is readable by whoever holds it.

sync03 / 09

Sync

Lembaran doesn’t sync today. If sync is ever added, it will be optional, off until you turn it on, and it will go through your own iCloud account, where we can’t read it. This page will be updated before that version is released.

deleting04 / 09

Deleting your notes

Delete the app and your notes are deleted with it. Backups you exported yourself, and your iPhone’s own backups, stay wherever they are until you remove them.

purchases05 / 09

Purchases

The optional one-time “Pro” unlock is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. We never receive your payment details. Apple’s Privacy Policy governs that transaction.

system06 / 09

Widgets & system features

The home-screen widget reads your notes only from the same on-device storage, within a private app group on your device. Spotlight indexing and Siri Shortcuts are handled by iOS on your device and are not shared with us.

website07 / 09

About this website

This page is published on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records requests to it, including your IP address, under its own privacy statement.

That concerns this website, not the app. Lembaran itself never contacts this site, or any other.

children08 / 09

Children

Lembaran does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date before the change reaches the app.
