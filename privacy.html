Privacy Policy

Lembaran · last updated 7 July 2026

Lembaran collects nothing. Not “almost nothing” — nothing. No account, no analytics, no servers, no tracking. The detail is below, though there isn't much of it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We operate no servers that receive your data. The developer never sees your notes, because there is nowhere for them to go.

Where your notes live

Your notebooks and notes are stored locally on your device. Lembaran keeps automatic on-device backups so it can recover your notes if a file is damaged. If you choose to create a backup from Settings, the exported file is saved wherever you send it (for example your own iCloud Drive or Files) — that copy is governed by Apple's iCloud terms, not by us, and we have no access to it.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Pro” unlock is handled entirely by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We never receive your payment details. Apple's Privacy Policy governs that transaction.

Widgets & system features

The home-screen widget reads your notes only from the same on-device storage, within a private app group on your device. Spotlight indexing and Siri Shortcuts are handled by iOS on your device and are not shared with us.

Children

Lembaran does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
